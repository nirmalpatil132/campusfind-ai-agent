How it works

From "I lost it" to "got it back"

The AI does the paperwork and the searching. People make the final decision.

01

Tell the assistant

"I lost my black HP laptop bag in CSE block yesterday." It asks one short question at a time for anything missing.

02

Confirm the summary

You see exactly what will be saved. Nothing is saved until you reply yes.

03

Smart matching

A transparent scoring engine compares category, colour, place, time and brand, and shows potential matches with reasons.

04

Verified hand-over

The owner answers a private question. An admin compares it with the finder's hidden detail, then marks the item returned.

Trust & privacy

Built so the wrong person can't walk away with your things

Every safety rule is enforced on the server, whatever the AI says.

Private details stay hidden

The finder's secret detail is visible only to admins. Search results never show names, emails or phone numbers, and long numbers are masked automatically.

Humans verify, not the AI

A match is never treated as certain. Only an admin can verify ownership or mark an item returned. The AI has no button for that.

Honest about its AI

Runs on a free local language model (Ollama) or a clearly labelled rule-based engine. Every reply shows which engine wrote it.
